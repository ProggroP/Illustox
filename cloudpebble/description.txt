TITLE

Illustox


TAGLINE

A new hand-drawn-looking mountain landscape every hour, every quarter or every minute.


DESCRIPTION

Illustox paints flat, boho-style landscape illustrations on your wrist: layered mountain ridges, a sun by day and a crescent moon by night, and now and then a valley with a winding river. Every scene is generated on the watch, so you will hardly ever see the same picture twice. The only thing on top is the time, in a large, light typeface.

Pick one of six colour worlds or let every new scene choose its own. On Pebble Round 2 the landscape fills the round display, on Pebble Time 2 the full rectangle.


SETTINGS

- Colour world - Desert, Dusk, Sage, Ocean, Sunset, Candy, or Surprise me (a random world for every scene).
- New landscape - every minute, every 15 minutes or every hour.
- Shake for a new landscape - flick your wrist to paint a fresh scene right away.

12 or 24 hour format follows the watch setting.
